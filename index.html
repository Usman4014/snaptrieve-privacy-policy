Privacy Policy

Capture. Organize. Find.

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Snaptrieve ("we", "our", or "the App") is a screenshot organizer designed to help you organize, search, and manage screenshots on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Snaptrieve.

1. Information the App Handles

To provide its features, Snaptrieve processes information that you choose to add to the app, which may include:

  • Screenshots and images: Images you select or capture for importing into the app.
  • Extracted text: Text recognized from your screenshots using on-device text recognition.
  • Categories and related details: Category assignments, image hashes, timestamps, processing status, and other records associated with your saved screenshots.
  • App preferences: Settings and preferences saved locally on your device.

2. Local Storage and Offline Operation

Snaptrieve is designed to work offline. Your imported screenshots and related app records are stored locally on your device. The app uses local database and file storage to organize this information.

The developer does not operate a Snaptrieve cloud service or backend that receives your screenshots or extracted text. Snaptrieve does not require you to create an account.

3. On-Device Text Recognition

Snaptrieve uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition to recognize text in images. Text recognition is performed on your device as part of the app's screenshot-organizing functionality. Snaptrieve does not send your screenshot images or extracted text to a developer-operated server for OCR processing.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Crash Reporting

Snaptrieve does not currently integrate Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob. The app does not include developer-integrated advertising or analytics features.

5. Sharing and Exporting

If you choose to share an image or another item using your device's sharing options, the content you select is passed to the app or service you choose. That third party's handling of the information is governed by its own privacy policy.

6. Backup and Restore

Snaptrieve allows you to create a backup archive containing screenshot records, categories, related metadata, and image files. You choose where to save the backup and which backup file to restore.

Backups are not automatically uploaded to a Snaptrieve server. You are responsible for protecting exported backup files and for deleting them from their chosen storage location when they are no longer needed.

7. Device Permissions

Depending on the features you use and your device's operating system, Snaptrieve may request access to photos, images, camera capture, or files. These permissions are used to let you select or capture images and import, save, or restore backup files. You can manage app permissions through your device settings.

8. Third-Party Software

The app uses third-party software libraries to provide its functionality, including Google ML Kit for text recognition and platform components for image and file handling. Those libraries and the services you choose to use may be subject to their providers' terms and privacy policies.

9. Data Security

Your Snaptrieve content is stored locally on your device. Please use your device's available security features and keep exported backup files in a location you trust. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Snaptrieve provides in-app options to delete screenshots and clear application data.

Backup files exported outside the app are separate files. Deleting information inside Snaptrieve does not automatically delete previously exported backups; you must remove those files separately.

11. Children's Privacy

Snaptrieve is a general-purpose utility app and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account or ask users to submit personal information to a developer-operated service.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app or its data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
